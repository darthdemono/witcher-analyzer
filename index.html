Witcher Analyzer

A Witcher save is a 25 MB bag of quest files, item lists and area dumps, and the game never adds them up for you. Drop one in and you get the total: Geralt's level and gold, what he carries, every quest and where it stands, what he has learned to brew. Drop in a folder of saves and you get the order it all happened in.

One save, read in full.

Saves live in Documents\The Witcher\saves on Windows. Opening one is read-only, so pointing this at a live save is safe.
